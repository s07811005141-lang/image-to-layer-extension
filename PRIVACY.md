# Privacy — Image to Layer: Layer Split Examples

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This extension does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal or usage data.

- **No data collection.** It has no analytics, no tracking and no account. It makes no network requests while you browse the examples: every example and image ships inside the extension.
- **Search stays local.** What you type in the search box, and text you send to it with the right-click entry, is only used to filter the built-in examples on your device. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Right-click on an image** opens https://image2layers.org/app in a new tab. The image itself is not read, uploaded or passed to the site by the extension.
- **Permissions.** `contextMenus` only, for the two right-click entries.
- **Links.** Links to image2layers.org include UTM parameters (`utm_source=chrome-extension`) so the site can count visits from the extension. What happens on image2layers.org is covered by its own [privacy policy](https://image2layers.org/privacy-policy).
